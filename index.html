The Challenge

🌙 Lunar Extremes

Moon has -173°C nights, 127°C days, radiation, and regolith. We need Earth places that simulate this to test habitats before launch.

🔴 Martian Conditions

Mars has thin CO2 atmosphere, dust storms, and perchlorate soil. Earth analogs help us prepare life-support systems.

🗺️ Data Gap

NASA data on analog sites is scattered across papers and agencies. No unified, searchable platform exists.

Our Solution - TerraNova Platform

Input: Moon/Mars mission parameters. AI matches NASA Earth observation data to rank best Earth analogs with similarity score.
